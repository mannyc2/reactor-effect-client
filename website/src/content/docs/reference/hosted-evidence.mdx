Every claim on this site about hosted Reactor rests on a paid run listed here. Each run is one
check of the [hosted harness](https://github.com/mannyc2/reactor-effect-client/tree/main/integration/hosted),
an Effect program over the public API, run by the maintainer against hosted H3 Reference Turbo
Realtime (`reactor/h3-reference-to-video-turbo-realtime`), the `avatar`, `character` and `rejoin` checks
against Vidu S2-Avatar (`reactor/vidu-s2-avatar`), and `fasth3` against FastH3 (`reactor/fast-h3`).
Its evidence file and summary are
committed under `integration/hosted/evidence/`, with the session ids, so Reactor can find its side
of each run.

<Aside type="note" title="Hosted and rehearsed are kept apart">
  CI runs every check as a rehearsal on `ReactorTest`, the simulated Reactor, and never allocates a
  paid session. A rehearsal shows the program is right about the contract it simulates; it is not
  evidence of what hosted Reactor does, and nothing on this page comes from one.
</Aside>

The harness refuses to run without `--i-authorize-paid-sessions` and a budget. Each run reserves
its worst case in a ledger before any token exists, and a ledger's total is capped at $10.

- **Estimates** are the evidence's own, at the rate Reactor's pricing stated then: 125 credits a
  second, $0.0125. Up to 0.7.0 they count every started minute whole, so they are upper bounds;
  from 0.8.0 they count seconds. On 2026-09-30 the pricing lists 350 credits a second, so the same
  sessions would cost 2.8 times the per-second estimates today. The `avatar`, `character` and `rejoin` runs
  are at Vidu S2-Avatar's 70 credits a second, $0.007, counted by the second. The published
  0.10.0 H3 runs and the FastH3 probe use 350 credits a second, $0.035, counted by the second.
- **Failures.** Each found cause was fixed before the check ran again to qualify. Most were the
  library's: 0.3.0-rc.0's acceptance, ICE pair and attach, 0.7.0's misplaced continued insert and
  doubled `stop`, and an `INACTIVE` session read as ended. Two were criteria of the checks' own
  making. The third 0.3.0-rc.0 vertical repeated the first on the same bytes, to settle billing and
  H3's reply order. Position zero, listed below, is H3's own ordering and is still unexplained.
  The first `avatar` run resumed no track at live and waited 10 s for an `end_call` that took
  13.2 s; the reconnect it then tried for the picture hung 82 s, past its own deadline. The first
  `character` run's connection failed with the native addon's `Protocol` failure 21 ms after the
  provider's first command, 6 ms after the connection was ready; its cause is not yet known, and
  the evidence now keeps the addon's own sentence for that failure, so a recurrence names its check.
  The 0.11.0 candidate attempt received HTTP 402 at its first H3 creation, before any session was
  allocated. No candidate phase ran, and FastH3 was not attempted. The evidence records no charge
  estimate and retains the $6.30 reservation. A second attempt, authorized separately, used a
  ledger of its own with a $10 limit and reserved $6.30. H3 passed all 15 of its criteria. FastH3's
  firm clip started 18.05 s after allocation, where H3's started at 12.38 s, and every later step
  followed that much later. FastH3's phase closed 80.05 s after allocation, past its fixed 80 s work
  deadline, so the run failed before its remaining criteria were judged. Both sessions' ends were
  confirmed, and the public Ledger was left empty. Hosted qualification remains pending.
- **Commits.** The 0.8.0-dev commits were renamed when the branch's messages were reworded, with no
  change to any tree; the ledger's summary maps each to its new name. The second `show`'s checkout,
  `c9f3f96`, matches the 0.8.0 release's library source except for its version string.

## Billing records

- The Reactor dashboard listed each session's duration to the second, matching the time from
  allocation to the `DELETE` within a second (0.8.0-api), and listed a 10-second session at 10 s,
  so Reactor meters by the second (0.8.0-probe).
- Whether the meter starts at creation or at `ready` stayed open: `ready` came 0.19–0.38 s after
  allocation, under the dashboard's one-second resolution.
- The balance readings fit neither the published rate nor whole minutes. 0.3.0-rc.0's third run
  moved the balance by $0.03–0.05, against $0.11 at the published rate; 0.8.0-dev's sessions had not
  moved it hours later; and the $1.09 between the readings around 0.8.0-probe fits neither.

## What the runs found about the platform

These are behaviours of hosted Reactor and its models the runs recorded, worth knowing whatever
client you use.

**FastH3** ([0.10.0-fasth3](https://github.com/mannyc2/reactor-effect-client/blob/main/integration/hosted/evidence/0.10.0-fasth3/summary.md),
2026-10-03): five comparable state/queue pairs had equal generation counts, four with an unbuilt
clip, and all 18 H3 state fields were present. Accepted length samples ranged from 5.167 to
14.375 s; off-grid samples rounded upward, while 5 and 15 s were refused. History kept at least
two clips; its capacity and eviction rule were unmeasured. With flush off, `clip_stopped` came
130 ms after `stop`, but frozen-tail timing was unmeasured, so this run does not establish the
documented drain time.

**Dropped H3 session** ([0.8.0-dropped](https://github.com/mannyc2/reactor-effect-client/blob/main/integration/hosted/evidence/0.8.0-dropped/summary.md),
2026-09-30): run `e87da9b1`, from checkout `b64aff33`, read `ACTIVE` through 4.21 s after
its owner was killed, then `INACTIVE` from 6.21 s through the last read at 60.21 s. Reactor had
not ended it; the API key ended it and confirmed `CLOSED`.

- H3 broadcasts the `queue_update` listing a new clip before the enqueue's correlated
  `clip_queued` reply (0.3.0-rc.0).
- An attached connection receives no media until it resumes its tracks, as Reactor's sessions page
  says (0.3.0-rc.0).
- The deployment reports version `v0.0.0`, where the documented schema is 0.5.5 (0.3.0-rc.0,
  0.3.1).
- A position-zero enqueue went ahead of the build in flight, where the schema says the running
  build is unaffected (0.6.0, 0.7.0). A queue read sent 1 ms behind an enqueue listed the new clip:
  commands applied in the order sent (0.7.0).
- `stop` names no clip and lands after its reply; under autoplay it skips to the next clip, so a
  second `stop` cuts that one (0.7.0).
- H3 flushes to black at each clip's end by default (`flush_on_clip_end`), one dark frame per seam
  (0.6.0, 0.7.0); holding the last frame removes it.
- A session whose last connection has gone reads `INACTIVE` and is still live (0.8.0-dev). One read
  `ACTIVE` for at least the first 2.1 s after its last connection closed, when the reads stopped
  (0.8.0-api); another, first read 8.4 s after its owner was killed, was `INACTIVE` (0.8.0-dev).
- Content moderation's `terminate` verdict came 0.40–1.01 s after the flagged enqueue, which had
  been answered normally, and named no category, input, command or request (0.8.0-dev, 0.8.0-api).
- A 16,171-character prompt was accepted, then failed by `clip_failed` 2.39 s later: H3's text
  budget is enforced at build (0.8.0-api).
- A clip recording came back `ClipReady` in 2.6 s, but its download timed out at 15.1 s
  (0.8.0-api).
- A session allocated and never connected read `ACTIVE` throughout and was ended by Reactor at its
  60-second cap, 60.09 s after allocation; a second create on a spent single-session token was
  refused with 403 (0.8.0-probe).
- Vidu S2-Avatar sent a call's picture only once `main_video` was resumed after the call went live;
  on the same connection, the next call's picture came with nothing done. Its voice came without
  that resume (0.9.2-avatar).
- Vidu S2-Avatar acknowledged a refused command, and its `command_error`, which names the command
  but no request, came first. A command carrying an explicit `null` and a `say` with no text got an
  `invalid_command` error frame (0.9.2-avatar).
- Vidu S2-Avatar answered `end_call` 6.9–13.2 s after it was sent, past the SDK's 10-second reply
  deadline on the first run (0.9.2-avatar).
- Vidu S2-Avatar declares `clone_voice` and refused it with `CLONING_DISABLED`; `list_voices` listed
  56 voices, and `call_max_seconds` read 7200 (0.9.2-avatar).
- A connection that replaced a Vidu S2-Avatar call's own during the call carried the session's
  messages, the state each second and the transcripts, but none of the call's picture or voice, with
  both tracks resumed while the call was live (the first 0.9.2-avatar run).

## Not yet run on hosted Reactor

- **Most of 0.9.0.** One paid run has exercised 0.9.0: the one-session `showreel` check, from a
  checkout with the published linux-x64 addon. `Playout.place` and `follows`, 0.9.0's renewal and
  its other checks have run only on `ReactorTest`, and its published client packages have not run on
  hosted Reactor.
- **The `turn` check**, the vertical from a network that blocks outbound UDP so a relay must carry
  the media. Every paid run went over a direct path.
- **`reactor-effect-browser`.** No paid run has used the browser host.
- **The in-process native host under Node.** It ran under Bun, and Node ran only the isolated host.
  The quickstart and the terminal viewer use it when run with `node`.
- **macOS arm64.** Every paid run was on Linux x64.
- **Long sessions.** The longest paid run lasted under two minutes; no multi-hour renewal soak has
  run.
- **The avatar example, and more of Vidu S2-Avatar.** The provider ran in the `character` and `rejoin`
  checks, not through the example program. A caller's microphone or webcam and reference images
  have not run. Whether Reactor ends a Vidu S2-Avatar session at its cap is open: on the first run
  nothing read the session between its last message, at 81 s, and the key's end at 164 s.
- **Restreaming and native publication.** No run has sent native media on to a browser, to RTMP or
  to LiveKit.
